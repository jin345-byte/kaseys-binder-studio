Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 31, 2026

Kasey's Binder Studio is a Pokémon card binder planning application. This policy explains what information the application uses and how it is handled.

Information we collect

If you use Binder Studio as a guest, binder data is stored locally in your browser or device. If you choose to sign in with Google, Binder Studio receives basic account information provided by Google, such as your Google account identifier, email address, display name, and profile picture.

Binder data

Signed-in users may have binder layouts, pages, settings, and related application data synchronized to Cloudflare D1 so the same Binder Studio account can access saved binders across devices.

How information is used

Information is used only to authenticate users, maintain user sessions, save and synchronize binder data, and provide Binder Studio functionality. Binder Studio does not sell personal information.

Google Sign-In

Google authentication is provided by Google. Binder Studio does not receive or store your Google password.

Data storage and security

Account and synchronized binder data may be stored using Cloudflare services. Reasonable technical safeguards are used, including secure session cookies and server-side authentication checks. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

Data retention and deletion

Guest data can be removed by clearing the browser's site data. Signed-in account data may remain stored until it is deleted from the service. A user may request deletion of their Binder Studio account and associated cloud-synchronized data by contacting the site operator.

Third-party services

Binder Studio may use third-party services to provide authentication, hosting, card data, and artwork loading. Those services may process information according to their own privacy policies.

Children

Binder Studio is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as Binder Studio changes. The updated date at the top of this page will be revised when material changes are made.

Contact

For privacy or account deletion questions, contact the Binder Studio site operator through the contact information provided with the application.
